Before you move.

Do you have homes in every city?

Our home catalogue is in Rome. In the European cities listed here, BOOM offers tenant-side rental support. You can choose help with your search, an application, or an attended viewing.

When do I pay for European support?

The timing depends on your city and service. Select your city above, then read the current payment conditions on its original BOOM service page before ordering.

Is my rent included in the service fee?

No. The prices shown are BOOM service fees, including VAT. Rent, deposits and other costs relating to a property are separate. Review the chosen service’s scope and terms before ordering.

Can I explore before sharing my details?

Yes. You can browse the homes and review the European services first. When you are ready, continue to the original BOOM listing or desk page.
