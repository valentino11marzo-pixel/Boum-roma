Before you move.

Do you have homes in every city?+

The BOOM homes catalogue is in Rome. In the other cities shown here, BOOM provides tenant-side rental search and support. Selecting a European desk does not open a local BOOM property catalogue.

What will I pay?+

Choose your city to see current service fees. Read the original city page for the full conditions before you order.

What if the search isn’t right for me?+

The conditions depend on your city and service. Review the original city page before you order.

Who do I speak with?+

Valentino Egidi, founder of BOOM. The agency is Egidi Immobiliare S.r.l., based at Via dei Coronari 181/184, 00186 Roma. You can find the desk’s contact options and hours on its original BOOM page.

Can I browse without choosing dates?+

Yes. You can choose a city, read the service and explore the Rome homes before providing dates or personal details. Any enquiry or order continues on the original BOOM service.
