FAQ

Good to know.

How does the BOOM referral work?
Share BOOM with someone moving to Rome. When they rent through BOOM, you both receive €50 — them as a discount, you as a credit or payout.
Who can I refer?
Anyone moving to Rome who needs housing — friends, classmates, colleagues, or incoming students and staff.

After your referral completes their booking and moves in. We confirm by email or WhatsApp.

No — refer as many people as you like. Every successful referral is rewarded.
