# Launching a BOOM city desk

A desk is DATA plus a registry entry: `cities/<slug>.json` → `build-cities.mjs` → `/<slug>.html`, and `api/_market.js` `CITY_MARKETS.<slug>` for the server (lead door, Stripe, webhook, Telegram card). Read CLAUDE.md «BOOM Cities» (v1.6 «il desk aviazione completo», v1.7 «il biglietto v1.7») and «BOOM Desks» (the hub, `/desks`, and «La mappa» — the Horizon) first. The build refuses anything false or off-budget: let it tell you what is missing.

A new desk is never one page: the hub `/desks` and every other desk's Network map are built from the same JSONs, so they change with it (see §5b).

## 0 · Where the code lives (v1.7, Stage 0)
- The served assets `assets/boom-city.css|js`, `boom-onda.js`, `boom-desks.css|js`, `boom-map.js` are EMITTED by `node build-cities.mjs` from the commented sources in `assets/src/` (comments stripped; `?v=` and the weights are those of the emitted files). Edit ONLY `assets/src/*`; never a served file by hand — the next build overwrites it and `node build-cities.mjs --check` exits 1 on a stale copy. A desk launch normally touches no asset at all.
- Budgets (`WEIGHT_KB`, `BUDGET`, `HUB_WEIGHT_KB`, `HUB_BUDGET`, `MAP_KB` in `build-cities.mjs`) are never raised to fit: cut copy or craft, and say so in the PR.

## 1 · Decide (write the decisions down before coding)
- slug (English: `london`, `milan`), label = JSON `city` exactly, Italian label for operator strings (`labelIt`)
- currency (EUR default; non-EUR needs `currency` in CITY_MARKETS **and** the JSON), prices (the grid is 500 / 199 / 149 in the desk's currency)
- mode `search` (sells, checkout) or `waitlist` (no till, no Stripe)
- desk hours and zone: `desk.timeZone` (IANA; `Europe/Milan` does not exist → `Europe/Rome` + `desk.zoneName`), the holidays key
- the map: `iata` (3 capital letters, the airport-style code the boards and the map print) and `geo` `{lat, lon}`, the city's real coordinates. The Horizon FRAMES ITSELF: the camera (`HZ_CAM`, above the Sahara) fits every desk plus Rome on every page, so a new desk re-frames the map and nothing is refused by a fixed rectangle — there is no `mapXY`, no `HUB_VIEWBOX`, no `BOW_FIRST` to touch. `validGeo` still wants lat 35–60, lon −11–30 and a point the camera can see (a far desk zooms the map OUT freely and re-frames it; `HZ_SCALE_MAX` only stops the fit zooming IN past the horizon when few desks are drawn; a desk the camera cannot see is refused by `validGeo`, never by the scale). The coast in `cities/_europe.json` is kept in degrees for the window `[-45, 12, 70, 88]`: only a desk outside it needs `node design/pages-deco/genera-europa.mjs` with a wider window (the `.py` generator is gone)
- the desk's country (`country`, ISO upper) and whether it is the home country (Italy = same Italian rules as Rome, still a desk: derived, never a key)
- whether the region keeps an agents' REGISTRY (AICAT in Catalonia, RAICV in the Comunitat Valenciana, carte professionnelle in France, §34c GewO in Germany): then the Valencia pattern of §4b applies
- legal checklist to hand the founder (see «Known open items»)

## 2 · Server registry — `api/_market.js`
- `CITY_MARKETS.<slug>`: `label, labelIt, flag, country (ISO lower), holidays, currency, hoursEn, url, mode, checkout, plans: CITY_PLANS` (ONE shared plans object; `eur` = the amount in the desk's currency)
- phones: `DIAL` + a `nationalOf` branch for the country (keep the trunk 0 where E.164 keeps it, e.g. Italy; refuse shapes of other countries)
- `cityFromText` aliases come from `label` + `labelIt` automatically
- `cityReplyText` already adds «If you'd rather start now: <url>#pay-full-search» on every selling desk (the deep link opens the till on Full Search, Start-now never pre-ticked): nothing per desk to write

## 3 · Calendar — `js/desk-calendar.js`
- add a key with `fixed`, `easter` (always an array), and the rules the country needs (`mondays`, `weekdays` for substitute days; `dated` = a list of `YYYY-MM-DD` for a holiday decreed for ONE year only, e.g. Valencia's Sant Joan `['2026-06-24']` — it never recurs, so a later year must be added when the region publishes its decree); Italy is always unioned (the desk answers from Rome)
- a regional desk names its OWN key (`es-bcn`, `es-vlc`, `it-mi`, `gb-eng`) in `CITY_MARKETS.<slug>.holidays`: the country default (`es`) is Madrid's calendar and passes the "calendar exists" test silently — a wrong key is a wrong withdrawal date, not a failing build
- the file is inlined in every city page: keep it small, no `/*` inside strings
- pin every date for three years in `tests/cities/calendar.mjs`; note in CLAUDE.md the first year the region has NOT published yet (today: `es-vlc` 2028) so it is rechecked each spring

## 4 · The page — `cities/<slug>.json`
- copy the key structure of a selling desk: a Spanish desk copies `cities/madrid.json` (the build enforces the ES rules on `country: "ES"`: "Optional, chosen in writing" before any price — it is the fares head `#faresH` above the flagship ticket, and the build refuses a selling ES page whose `#faresH` does not come before the first `data-plan-price` in `#fares` —, the must-state verbatim, "Who pays the agent · LAU art. 20" as the first check), any other desk `amsterdam.json`; add `currency` if not EUR
- facts only from sources; no statistics, no market rents, no amounts other than the three plan prices (deposits in weeks/months of rent); banned phrases live in `tests/verita` and `build-cities.mjs` (CLOCK_RE, off-market, 500+, exclamation marks, a second rating, the aviation words, and the registration/licence guard: every sentence with that vocabulary must be listed in `cities/_claims.json`, the ALLOWED list — see §4b); «First class», an N° or a code on a ticket and the door's hover lift (`.door:hover` with a `translateY`) are refused by `tests/cities/run.mjs` (LEFTOVERS and the `.door:hover` check), NOT by the build: `node build-cities.mjs --check` stays green on them, only the suite bites
- 3–6 checks (the local law that protects the tenant; each `{id, title, at, text}`, `at` is the short legal label the hub table prints verbatim), exactly 8 papers, 3–6 FAQ including the free call and the refund rule
- the first card needs no JSON of its own: the flagship prints `pricing.plans[0].name` as its promise ("We search. You choose."), the situation lines are the `when` fields, the conditions are `condition`; the economy pair, the coupon, the barcode and the pip are the build's
- the Departures steps carry a `status` each (`how.steps[i].status`: capitals, ≤ 12 characters, STATUS_RE; no SEAT/BOARDING/ON TIME/GATE/NOW words). Selling desks: `FREE`, `1 WORK DAY`, `IN PERSON`; waitlist desks: `FREE`, `WITH PARTNER`, `AT OPENING`
- v1.6 budgets (raised once by the founder on 03/11, never raised to fit): ≤ 640 visible words at load (prose ≤ 520, instruments ≤ 120 — the text inside `[data-instr]`), a text node ≤ 24 words, first phone screen ≤ 150, lead+leadStrong ≤ 30, fares ≤ 70, each ticket ≤ 60, per section departures 100 · pre-flight 60 · baggage 70 · gate 40 · network 40; weight ≤ 82 KB gz WITH the map counted (JS 14, CSS 15, onda 4, map 3.5 on its own line). Meta title ≤ 62, description 110–165
- the measured phone pins a desk must keep (ui.mjs): three prices WITH conditions readable at 390×664, 375×812 and 390×844; door and who-answers above the fold at 375×812 and 390×844. A longer situation line or lead pushes who-answers under 844 (Madrid is at 812: 32 px of room) — measure before writing

- `/desks` (`desks.html`, generated with the desks by the same `npm run build:cities`) reads every city JSON: a LIVE desk with `checkout: true` gets its board row (code, hours, clock, OPEN/CLOSED from `js/desk-calendar.js`), its arc, dot and label on the Horizon, its gate chip, its column in «Renting rules, city by city» and its `Service` in the hub's JSON-LD (identical to its own page's); a draft gets a «NOT OPEN» row and a hollow dot — never a link, a price, an hour or an arc. Counts and lists are tokens (`{cities}`, `{n}`): no hub copy to edit
- the hub REFUSES until: the desk's fares equal the other live desks' (label, price, condition, unit); `desk.person` is the same; every check `id` sits in one `rules.rows[].ids` of `cities/_hub.json` (add a new theme id to the right row); its «What we don't do» fact-sheet row says it does not list homes, represent/act for landlords, or promise a date; a fact a lawyer has not confirmed goes in `rules.hold` (`"<slug>:<id>": "<reason>"`, the hub shows the title only); the country has a name in `COUNTRY_NAME` (or its cell reads «—» with a warning)
- the hub's `dateModified` is the newest `updated` of the hub and the live desks: the `/desks` `<lastmod>` in `sitemap.xml` must equal it (the build refuses otherwise)
- **the Horizon** redraws on every desk page's Network and on the hub (arcs from Rome, hollow dots for desks not open, the coast under them, the same `d` on the hub and on the desk's own page). The build refuses three things, with three functions: `hzViolations` measures ONLY arc↔dot clearance — an arc within 6 px of a dot it does not serve (hollow dots and the hub ring included; the error names the pair, `ROM → XXX passes 4.2px from YYY`); `hzLabels` throws when a label finds no place (over another label, a dot, an arc or out of the frame: «network map: no place for the labels (…)»); `hzArcs` throws when no lift keeps an arc clear (of the other desks, the frame, the other arcs) OR the arc passes behind the horizon — the same message for both («network map: no bow takes the arc ROM → XXX clear of the other desks»): check `validGeo` of that desk before touching `HZ_LIFT`. The fix is the CAMERA or the LABELS, never a hidden dot: `HZ_CAM.pad` (the frame's margins; the fit is bound by the width) or `HZ_LIFT`/`HZ_LEAN` (the arcs' heights and sides, searched in order) for an arc; `HZ_FONT` (the phone/desk label boxes) or the placer's candidate sides for a label. The phone's pin spacing is asserted (`HUB_MAP_PX`: ≥ 44 px between live desks on the 390 and 320 phones, map 356 and 286 px wide; today AMS–LON is the closest pair, 55.6 and 44.7 px): a new desk close to an old one (Lyon next to Milan, Porto next to Madrid) is the case that bites
- the hub is NOT free: a live desk adds a board row (~10 words), a rules column (~14), a gate (2), a footer link (2), a brief word (1) — about 31 words against ~5 of headroom today (hub 975 / 980). The next desk (the seventh live) is therefore a STRUCTURAL decision (a shorter rules table, a board without the sub-lines, or a cap the founder raises once), not a line of copy; measure against `HUB_BUDGET` before writing (the lead counts with `{n}`, so it does not grow per desk)
- `python3 design/pages-deco/genera-og-cities.py --hub` when the live set changes (`og-desks.png` prints the live codes)
- weights: the hub ≤ 78 KB gz (page 28 — the coast is ~3 KB of it); a desk page ≤ 82 WITH the map asset (3.0 KB today) counted; at the cap, copy and craft give the bytes, never the caps
