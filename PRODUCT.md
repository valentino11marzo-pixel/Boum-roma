# BOOM

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People looking for a home in Rome, including people moving from abroad and
students choosing around a campus or everyday destination. People using the
European desks need tenant-side rental search and support for a chosen city.
These audiences and journeys are present in the existing public site; this
record does not introduce a new audience or commercial position.

## Product Purpose

Help a visitor understand a real home, its location, costs and availability,
or choose an appropriate support service and continue into its original form.
Flight is an optional second-home experience for the existing BOOM project.

## Positioning

The existing Rome journey connects real listings, Skyline, an everyday
destination, cost information and the original application. European desks
offer their own configured services. The founder and agency are identifiable.
These are capabilities in the project, not claims about comparative performance.

## Operating Context

Visitors can explore before providing dates, then carry valid public preferences
through homes, details and original forms. The site distinguishes Rome listings
from tenant-side services in other cities. Private places must not enter URLs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript on Vercel, with existing original controllers and
server handlers. Firestore is the sole customer-data authority. Do not duplicate
forms, catalogue, payment logic or customer state for a design alternative.
Current products, prices, terms, city status and legal identity come from the
actual site/server configuration. No invented offices, customers or metrics.
Preview work stays on its development branch; main is merged by Valentino.

## Brand Commitments

The name and original BOOM mark, black/yellow identity, original Solari and
geographic globe are established project assets. Valentino explicitly rejected
the substituted Instrument Sans and asked to recover the original weight300;
CLAUDE.md identifies that display voice as Helvetica Neue300, with Inter fallback.
His latest brief authorizes materially different futuristic aviation directions,
meticulous detail and new opportunities, while respecting the real BOOM identity.
On 8 November he explicitly reaffirmed the original pulsating Solari background as
an element to retain. Refine its placement and intensity around the destination;
do not remove or substitute that signature in a later redesign.
He then rejected the porcelain surround and rounded outer frame. New Dispatch
compositions should open the scene while retaining the original Solari pulse.
He has delegated routine design and implementation decisions in this conversation.

## Evidence on Hand

`CLAUDE.md` documents brand and architecture. `index.html` is the existing main
homepage. `/assets/boom-brand-lockup.svg` and its ink version are actual marks.
Flight has the original Solari, local Natural Earth geography, the real listing
catalogue and four Rome service doors. Company and service claims must stay
within the actual source. No new photo/video shoot is available for this work.
On 11 November Valentino confirmed Rebecca and Fausto's operations in Spain
and the Netherlands, Daniele's in the Netherlands and England, and his own
coordination from Rome with travel across capitals. BOOM also works with partner
agencies and owners. This confirms country coverage, not city offices or languages.

## Product Principles

- Make the visitor's next useful action clear.
- Connect visual ambition to real capabilities.
- Keep scope, costs and conditions visible before commitment.
- Preserve valid preferences and user control of motion.
- Distinguish demonstrated behavior from unmeasured conversion hypotheses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing implementation supports keyboard navigation, reduced motion,
explicit pause, readable contrast and narrow phones. Preserve these paths.
