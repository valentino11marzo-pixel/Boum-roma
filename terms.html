4. Commission & Fees

4.1 Standard Commission
BOOM charges a one-time commission, due at signature of the lease, of either:

  • One month's rent, OR
  • 10% of the total annual rent (whichever is lower)

The commission is subject to Italian VAT (IVA) at the statutory rate, currently 22%, which is added to the amounts above. Example: monthly rent €1,200 → commission €1,200 + 22% IVA = €1,464. If you have paid the Property Finding fee (§4.2), the €350 already paid is deducted from this total (€1,464 − €350 = €1,114 due at signing).

4.2 Property Finding Service
PFS costs €350, fixed, IVA included. Payment is by card via Stripe to Egidi Immobiliare S.r.l. and is receipted.

Within 24 hours of payment we hold a briefing call and confirm your search criteria to you in writing (budget ceiling, areas, number of bedrooms, move-in window, must-haves). Those written criteria are the sole benchmark for the guarantee below.

Guarantee. If, within 15 calendar days from the written confirmation of your criteria, we have not presented you at least 3 properties matching all of them, the €350 is refunded in full. The refund is automatic: you do not need to request it. We issue it to the same card via Stripe within 5 working days of the 15th day; your bank may take a further 5–10 working days to show it on your statement. No admin fee or deduction of any kind applies.

The guarantee is met when 3 matching properties are presented, whether or not you choose to rent one of them. Declining properties that match the written criteria does not trigger the refund; the search nonetheless continues at no extra cost until you sign a lease or ask us to stop. The 15 days is the refund trigger, not the end of the service.

On success the €350 is deducted in full from the commission in §4.1.
