# Spain — reading a residential lease (LAU) for a desk client

Built on the first Madrid case (November 2026). It is a reading list, not legal advice: every legal sentence we write to a client or a landlord names its source, and anything read from a commentary instead of the BOE is marked "unverified" in the note to the operator (boe.es is often unreachable from the sandbox).

**Check first, every time:** the status of the housing decree RDL 29/2026 (in force 8 October 2026; its validation vote was due on 12 November 2026). Several protections below came from it or were changed by it. If its fate is unknown the day you write, put the protection **in the lease itself** — then it holds whatever Parliament did.

## 0 · Before the clauses
- [ ] Is it a **vivienda** lease (the tenant's permanent home, LAU art. 2) or a *temporada* lease (art. 3.2)? A seasonal label on a real home does not remove the tenant's rights, but say which one the paper claims and why it matters for duration.
- [ ] Is the landlord a **person or a company**? Minimum stay with extensions is 5 years for a person, 7 for a company (art. 9.1).
- [ ] Is the draft **somebody else's lease** with the name changed (previous tenant's dates, "continues in force", joint liability with a stranger, furniture that is not there)? That was the first case. The fix is a NEW lease from the real start date.
- [ ] Cadastral use = residential; floor and door equal to the listing; size plausible (Sede Electrónica del Catastro, public data, no login).

## 1 · The parties and the home
- [ ] The tenant alone (or exactly the people who will live there), name as on the passport, NIE/passport number, current contacts. No "solidaria" liability with anyone not signing.
- [ ] The landlord as on the land registry; for a company: CIF, registered office, who signs and by what power.
- [ ] The home: address with floor and door, cadastral reference, furnished or not (an "unfurnished" flat with a furniture clause is a deposit dispute waiting).
- [ ] Occupants, pets, subletting, use (home only). If the client has a pet, the clause says **is allowed**, not "would be allowed".

## 2 · Duration and leaving
- [ ] Start date = the day of the keys. Extensions by law up to 5/7 years (art. 9), then yearly up to 3 more (art. 10).
- [ ] **Leaving early (art. 11)**: after six months, with 30 days' notice. The lease may set an indemnity of one month of rent per year left (pro rata). Ask for *no indemnity*; if refused, the legal maximum is the ceiling. Write the earliest exit date in the email to the client.
- [ ] The landlord's "need" clause (art. 9.3) only works for a PERSON and only if written in the lease. A sale does not end the lease inside the 5/7 years (art. 14).
- [ ] Late move-out penalties: read the number; say it.

## 3 · Rent and its updates
- [ ] Amount, due date (LAU default: first seven days of the month, art. 17.2), payment by transfer to an account **in the owner's name**.
- [ ] The first, partial month: the exact pro-rata figure, written.
- [ ] **Update**: once a year, on the anniversary of the lease, only if the lease says so (art. 18). Index: for leases signed after Ley 12/2023 came into force (late May 2023) the cap is the INE's rent index (IRAV). A draft that updates "every 1 December with the IPC" is an old template.
- [ ] No rent rise for improvements inside the first 5/7 years unless agreed (art. 19).

## 4 · Deposit and guarantees
- [ ] **Fianza**: one month of rent (art. 36.1), lodged by the landlord with the regional body (Madrid: Agencia de Vivienda Social; Catalonia: INCASÒL; Valencia: Generalitat). Ask for the receipt of the lodging.
- [ ] **Additional guarantee**: at most two months of rent for leases up to 5/7 years (art. 36.5). It covers debts or proven damage. It is not a penalty: strike wording that lets the landlord "keep it" for leaving.
- [ ] Return: with interest after one month from handing back the keys (art. 36.4). Write "within one month".

## 5 · Costs
- [ ] The landlord's agency and contract costs are the landlord's (art. 20.1, Ley 12/2023): a draft that puts them on the tenant gets corrected. (BOOM's own fee is the tenant's choice, optional and in writing, as the desk pages say; it never appears in anything sent to the landlord.)
- [ ] Community fees, IBI, waste tax: the tenant pays them only if the lease says so **with the annual amount at the date of the lease** (art. 20.1). A new tax starts from the bill of the year the tenant lives there, pro rata.
- [ ] Utilities with individual meters: the tenant's (art. 20.3); who holds the contracts today, and the change of holder after the keys.

## 6 · Repairs and works
- [ ] The landlord keeps the home habitable without raising the rent (art. 21.1); small repairs from ordinary use are the tenant's (art. 21.4). A clause putting "all repairs" on the tenant is against the law: ask for the legal wording.
- [ ] Works by the tenant only with written consent (art. 23). Drilling, TV brackets: ask in writing first.

## 7 · Rights that a clause cannot remove
- [ ] Any clause that changes the rules of Title II to the tenant's harm is void (art. 6). So an old clause that cannot be used against this tenant can be **left alone**: say so to the client, do not make the landlord rewrite it. Fewer changes, faster yes.
- [ ] Pre-emption (tanteo y retracto, art. 25): a waiver in the lease was allowed before the 2026 decree; check its status.
- [ ] Energy performance certificate: the landlord hands a copy (RD 390/2021). Ask for it and for the heating type.

## 8 · The handover
- [ ] Keys on the start date, never "already delivered" on the signing date.
- [ ] Inventory with dated photos and meter readings, signed by both on the day (the Anexo I of this kit).
- [ ] If the lease says the landlord "has received" the money at signing, the client pays at the table, after the three checks (`templates/before-you-pay-en.md`).

## 9 · What we ask, and how much
Six essential corrections at most, each tied to an article above or to money about to move. Then the small things of law in one paragraph. Everything else stays. The rule the client gets: **if one essential correction is refused, don't sign** — and a Full Search goes on.
