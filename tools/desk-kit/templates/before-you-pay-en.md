# Before you pay — three checks, the table, the transfers (English, for the client)

## Three checks. No transfer until all three are done
1. **Ownership.** The land-registry extract shows [LANDLORD / COMPANY] as the owner, with no seizures (a mortgage alone is normal). [We're getting it for you. / Ask the landlord for a recent one.]
2. **Authority.** Whoever signs can sign: the ID equals the owner on the extract, or, for a company, the page of the deed appointing the administrator or an extract from the companies register. A page showing who FOUNDED the company is not that.
3. **The bank account.** A bank certificate showing the holder [OWNER NAME] and exactly the IBAN in the lease. Call the landlord and have the IBAN read to you. If your bank shows the payee's name when you type the IBAN, it must be the owner's. Never accept a change of account by email.

## At the table
- Compare every printed page with the PDF you received, including the IBAN.
- Corrections by hand on both copies; both of you initial each one.
- Both sign every page. The date is the real date.

## The payment
Only after the three checks and the signatures, from your own account, never in cash:
- [deposit: AMOUNT] — reference «[Deposit ADDRESS]»
- [extra guarantee: AMOUNT] — reference «[Extra guarantee ADDRESS]»
- [first rent, DATES: AMOUNT] — reference «[Rent DATES ADDRESS]»

Use instant transfers if your bank offers them, so the landlord sees the money arrive, and check your daily transfer limit the day before. Keep every receipt.

## If a check is still open the day before
Tell us straight away. Don't sign "to keep the flat": a landlord who is the owner can show it in a day.
