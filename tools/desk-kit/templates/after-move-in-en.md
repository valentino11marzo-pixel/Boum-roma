# After the keys — the first weeks, and how to leave (English, for the client)

## The day of the keys
- Print two copies of the inventory (Annex I). Go room by room with dated photos, write the meter numbers and readings, test the appliances, the heating, the hot water and both sets of keys. Both sign; email the photos to the landlord the same day.

## The first days
- Put electricity, water and gas in your name, with the readings from the inventory.
- Home insurance with liability cover: under most leases you answer for damage you cause to others (a leak to the flat below).
- Register at the town hall at the new address ([padrón / BRP / Anmeldung / Anagrafe]) with the signed lease.

## The first weeks
- The receipt of the deposit lodged with [THE PUBLIC BODY]: the landlord must give it to you. If it doesn't come by [DATE], ask.
- Rent is due [WITHIN THE FIRST N DAYS] of each month. Set a standing order.
- Ask in writing before drilling or fixing anything to the walls.

## Leaving
- Notice: [30 days] in writing, [by registered letter to the landlord's address in the lease, or by email with a written reply].
- Earliest exit date under your lease: [DATE].
- The deposit comes back [within one month] of handing back the keys, against the inventory you signed today.

## From us
Your BOOM file stays open: write to the desk with any letter from the landlord or the building you don't understand.
