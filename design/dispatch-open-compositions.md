# Dispatch: three openings without the frame

8 November 2026. The porcelain surround and rounded instrument enclosure were
rejected; the original pulsating Solari was explicitly retained. This is a
composition study inside BOOM's existing identity, presented in the existing
Studio with the actual destination, catalogue, services and journey.

## Shared visual language

The entrance is black (#000), connected to the black navigation. Native Solari
yellow (#ffd700), white (#fff), silver geography and supporting text (#d1d1c9)
retain their existing jobs. Helvetica Neue300 carries the display voice; the
existing UI family, controls and validators remain. The pale lower chapters
and company footer retain their established role as reading surfaces.

The outer panel has no radius, outline, shadow or porcelain moat. Spacing
separates the destination and geographic scene. The original canvas lives in
the heading aperture, fades before actual city controls and never receives
pointer input. Pause, reduced motion and offscreen lifecycle remain native.

## Compositions

- **Aperta / open**: the left destination and six-city directory face a large
  unframed globe on the right. This is the default entrance and the shortest
  route from the mechanical city word to the visitor's next action.
- **Atlante / atlas**: the destination and action form a wide header; a horizontal
  city directory precedes a full-width geographic horizon. The globe reads as
  geography at page scale, with its native controls kept accessible. The wide
  aperture uses a larger horizon through the original shared projection for
  drawing, city targets and flight origin; Full view and phones keep the native
  framing.
- **Concourse / concourse**: the original mechanical board becomes a wide
  departure marquee, above the city/action area and the geographic scene.
  Hierarchy comes from scale and alignment rather than an enclosing object.
- **Precedente / instrument**: the prior porcelain frame remains explicitly
  selectable for comparison. It is no longer the Dispatch default.

On phones, content returns to a single column with real city targets, visible
actions and readable controls. The same nodes are laid out differently: there
is no extra form, map, catalogue, canvas, animation loop or customer store.

## Comparison and continuity

`composition` is an allowlisted, public presentation parameter of Dispatch.
The existing Studio offers all four compositions, desktop/phone sizing and a
full-page link. Switching uses the existing valid-draft capture; invalid dates
must keep the original fields and error. Private places remain excluded.

Explicit composition follows Flight navigation, including listing review and
return to the home. Original city desks and application forms receive only
their existing journey data. Other visions do not adopt these compositions.
The homepage at `/` is unchanged; this remains a branch Preview.

These are design alternatives, not evidence of a measured conversion uplift.
