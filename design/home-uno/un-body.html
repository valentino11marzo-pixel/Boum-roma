Beat 1 · The schedule

Rome is read every fifteen minutes.

The portals' own alert mail is opened on the quarter hour, day and night, and the listing behind each tracking link is rebuilt and read. Your search is regenerated every night; a briefing on the last 48 hours is written at dawn.

Beat 2 · The scale

Everything found gets weighed.

Budget carries fifty points, bedrooms thirty, district twenty. The weights are printed here because a score you cannot inspect is just marketing — now you can argue with the result, which is the point.

50 + 30 + 20 = 100
Beat 3 · The knife

Too far over budget? Instant no.

Up to twenty per cent over your ceiling scores half — Roman rents are negotiable, and we'd rather show you a close call than hide it. Past that line it's a veto, however beautiful the flat. And agency relistings are stored, never pushed: we won't send you to pay someone else's commission.

Veto past +20% · agencies never pushed

Most of it, you never see.
