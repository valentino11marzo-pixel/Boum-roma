## Comparison and tonight's decision

Use the same customer tasks for each candidate: understand what BOOM does, choose
a city, choose appropriate support, find the exact cost and scope, identify who answers,
and reach the original next step. Compare desktop, a narrow phone and the user's actual
viewport. Judge legibility, interaction effort, trust evidence, brand distinctiveness,
motion control and performance. A technically correct page is not automatically the
winning design; equally, a beautiful image is not a tested journey.

Build one convincing candidate per track first, keep one serious alternative each,
then choose one coherent page system for final detail. Do not combine every interesting
element into the homepage. Conversion and SEO uplift remain hypotheses until measured.
Tonight's launch is conditional on a complete verified path and Valentino's main merge;
do not promise a deadline or promote an unreviewed Preview to production.
