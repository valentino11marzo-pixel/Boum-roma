# BOOM Panorama — aviation identity restored, 5 November 2026

The 6 November service integration in `panorama-live-ticketing.md` restores the four original service colour families and ticket identities. The black/yellow hero and city direction below remains binding.

## Binding direction

Valentino rejected the grey refinement: restore strong BOOM black/yellow, make
Solari the hero, identify each city through an architectural detail and develop
the services as aviation tickets. This overrides generic advice to mute the brand.
Keep the readable interface type, real prices and working journeys.

- Canvas #000; surface #080808; raised #15140c; text #fffef7;
  secondary #d3d0c4; brand/action/selected #ffd700.
- Original yellow Solari and its physical flap typography lead the heading.
  Instrument Sans remains the reading/control family, not the hero identity.
- Selected city and service are black on solid yellow, plus text/pressed state.
- Service: black content body, genuine process line, yellow price/action stub.
  One dashed seam and small notches express the ticket, without invented flights,
  barcodes, availability, status or decorative fake data.
- Desktop ticket: body | perforation | price/action. Phone ticket: body above
  perforation above price/action. Terms remain readable and tied to each offer.
- Restore the original six architectural signatures in the globe header. Europe
  owns the SVG/caption updates; no second city state, new imagery or new claims.
  The original Solari field responds to the existing city/motion controller.

The emotional temperature is crisp and energetic. Yellow owns meaningful regions
instead of being reduced to a faint accent. No additional colour per city: city
identity comes from its architecture, name and original motion pattern.
