How BOOM works

Half portal. Half agency. Then a concierge.

Most platforms show you listings and leave. Most agencies show you three flats and their own interests. BOOM is built as both halves at once — the software of a portal where software is honest, the accountability of a licensed agency where a person must answer — and after the keys, we stay.

One platform · three jobs

What each half does for you.

P The portal

Browse like software.

Everything a portal should be — with the tricks removed.

  • Real availability dates — and when we don't know, the page says “ask us”, never “available now”.
  • Honest map pins — exact only where the address is verified; otherwise the page says it's approximate.
  • Skyline — the whole catalogue on Rome from above, with walk-times to the places of your life.
  • Hold a home online — €300, refundable, 48 hours off the market while you decide.
A The agency

Signed like a professional.

A licensed Rome agency stands behind every line.

  • Every home walked in person before it reaches the catalogue — never a stranger's photos.
  • A registered contract (L.431/98) with the Agenzia delle Entrate — signed from your phone.
  • One fee, written first — 10% of the annual rent, once. Every euro through Stripe, receipted.
  • Deposit filmed in & out — its state is a recording, not an argument.
C The concierge

Landed like a local.

The part that starts where the others finish.

  • A named human who replies on WhatsApp within 2 hours — not a form, not a queue.
  • The home's manual in your pocket — Wi-Fi, heating, waste days, who to call.
  • Your landing handled — codice fiscale, utilities, residency: the Welcome to Rome guide walks you through.
  • Rent that runs itself — card or SEPA, a receipt for every payment, maintenance from your portal.
