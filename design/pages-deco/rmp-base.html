FAQ

Good to know.

When should I book?
4–8 weeks before you land is ideal — September fills first. Once you send a shortlist, the first live video viewing is usually scheduled within 48 hours.
I haven't found apartments yet — can I still buy it?
Yes. Send your budget, zones and move-in date and we point you to live listings — from the portals and from BOOM's own verified homes. The viewings and the contract check then apply to whichever homes you pick. If you'd rather we run the entire hunt, Property Finding (€350) is the full service.
What exactly is included?
Two live video viewings (HD photos + honest report each), full Deal Assistance on the home you choose — clause-by-clause contract review in English, landlord & ownership verification, negotiation — plus arrival setup: utilities plan, codice fiscale guidance and a priority WhatsApp line until you're in. Bought separately that's €427+.
What if the first apartment fails the check?
Then it did its job — you just avoided a bad deal from 2,000 km away. We tell you honestly, and the contract review moves to the next home: the pack covers the one you actually sign for.
Is this for students?
Perfect for students and for parents abroad — JCU, LUISS, AUR, Sapienza arrivals. We schedule viewings across time zones and copy your parents on the report if you want.
Refunds?
A viewing we cannot deliver (advertiser unreachable) is refunded. And if you rent one of BOOM's own homes, the €299 is credited toward your agency fee.
