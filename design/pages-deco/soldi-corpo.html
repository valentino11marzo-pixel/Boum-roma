The rails

How the money travels.

Stripe, with a receipt for every line

Card checkout, never a bank transfer to a private person, never cash in an office. Each payment lands with its own receipt in your inbox.

A licensed counterparty

The company on the receipt is Egidi Immobiliare S.r.l., a licensed Rome agency — BOOM® is its EU trade mark 019317594. You always know who holds your money.

What never passes through us

Utilities (electricity, gas, water, internet) and the registration tax. Both go to the providers and to the State — we never touch them, and we never mark them up.

The hold optional · €300

Only if you need the home to stop moving while you decide. 48 hours off-market, one tap, refundable.

Final walkthrough against the move-in footage, keys back, deposit returned. The way it should always have worked.
