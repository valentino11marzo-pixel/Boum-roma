# BOOM: three genuinely different visual directions

6 November 2026. Valentino rejected Panorama/Terminal because rearranging the
same black panels did not provide a different visual language. He also rejected
the replacement display face. This request authorizes new hypotheses while
preserving the actual BOOM identity and product, and explicitly delegates design
choices. This round therefore delivers three working alternatives for review.
It does not select a new production homepage or rebrand the whole project.

## Pinned identity

The actual reference is Helvetica Neue300, documented in CLAUDE.md and the
original shared stylesheet. Instrument Sans400–700 cannot express that voice.
New visions and Studio recover the original family:300 display,400 reading,
500 controls. The actual BOOM mark, yellow #FFD700, Solari and geography remain.
The old A/B versions remain historical comparisons with their original styling.
Helvetica is not bundled or redistributed; the original fallback stack remains.

## Research and hypotheses

The following are design inferences, not claims of affiliation or measured uplift.

- [Pentagram, Vertical](https://www.pentagram.com/work/vertical): derive a
  coherent identity and motion vocabulary from the actual product and retained
  mark. BOOM's concentric mark, Solari, geography and real journey are the material.
- [Airbus, Airspace Explorer](https://www.airbus.com/en/innovation/innovation-ecosystem/airspace-explorer):
  technology integrated into a continuous cabin environment informed the light
  surface and adaptable scene, rather than a collection of instrument widgets.
- [KPF, Zayed International Airport](https://www.kpf.com/project/zayed-international-airport):
  continuous space and directional light informed transitions across actual
  geographic scales, keeping exits and next actions legible.

The seven grounded candidates were Airspace (luminous cabin), Livery (yellow
graphic field), Night Window (spatial geography), Concourse (continuous spatial
wayfinding), Route Book (travel folio), Connected City (actual urban map) and
Cutaway (Europe→Rome→home scales). They span architectural, graphic, spatial and
document traditions. Impeccable's seed:779793d5c46502c6 assigned Cutaway. Airframe
implements that layered journey on a light architectural surface. Signal is
the high-contrast graphic alternate; Orbit is the spatial alternate. The user's
delegation authorizes building the alternatives, not silently treating one as
their final preference. This session is code-led: actual responsive scenes and
controls are the review artifact; no standing workflow preference is stored.

The six external challengers were weighed against audience identification and
product clarity. Pastel mascot accessories, mandatory shareware wizard, paper
menagerie, audio meters and hand-drawn zine were declined: their literal grammar
conflicts with pinned BOOM typography or needs facts/interactions this rental
journey does not have. The night-city system is competitive on spatial
identification; its actual-geography discipline supports Orbit without inventing
weather, household data or transit telemetry. The useful disciplines carried
forward are committed palettes, explicit validation, reversible interaction,
shared motion control and situational explanations; none imports costume motifs.

## Airframe

White/ink/yellow architecture. A broad original photograph scene follows a
lightweight city heading. The visitor switches the same scene between a real
home and the original Europe globe. The same valid planner follows the scene.
The service chapter is deliberately black, with light display typography and
horizontal ticket anatomy. Catalogue, detail, desks and footer use the same
light material rules. The risk is a generic white page: the real-media aperture,
physical Solari record and continuous composition must carry the design.

## Signal

The entire opening is BOOM yellow, with the original ink mark and large300
typography. A black spatial chapter and black planning strip create a strong
graphic rhythm. Services become broad manifests with a distinct scope and fare
stub. The risk is yellow overload: reading/form surfaces keep functional
contrast and the display voice stays light.

## Orbit

The globe becomes the room, with destinations on its edge, centered lightweight
destination type and a planning dock below. The actual geography remains
interactive, finite on destination entry, pausable and keyboard accessible.
The support chapter switches to light paper for reading and comparison. The
risk is another isolated globe widget: the scene must have breadth, minimal
chrome and a clear transition into homes or the actual city desk.

## New useful decisions

The real photographic rail and actual globe move into one scene without
cloning either. Scene choice is local presentation. Foreign cities show their
world view and original desk; they never inherit a Rome home photo claim.
Empty/failed photo content keeps the original truthful message and catalogue
access. No synthetic home or new photo/video asset is introduced.

The service helper asks where the visitor is in their move. Searching shows
Property Finding; a found home shows Virtual Viewing and Deal Assistance;
a contract shows Deal Assistance and Contract Check. All support restores all
four original nodes. It neither submits a request nor changes service prices,
terms, native links or credit conditions. City changes reset the presentation.

The old ornamental Solari field is absent in the visions, so its continuous
paint loop is also disabled. The actual Solari remains capable of finite flips;
the original globe owns its existing bounded camera. Scene changes use one
320ms response, cancelled by keyboard, pause, reduced motion and page exit.

## Boundaries and verification

`vision=airframe|signal|orbit` is presentation-only, whitelisted through Flight
navigation and native links. Studio uses one iframe and the existing public
draft validation. Original Apply, service and desk URLs exclude presentation
and private-place parameters. Forms, canvas, photo rail, catalogue and handlers
remain original. No dependency, tracking event or customer store is introduced.

The new browser scenarios verify each vision at1440/390, original node identity,
scene changes, visible geography, field lifecycle, exact300 type, actual service
guidance, native new tabs, home/detail/Back and Studio valid/invalid drafts.
Existing Panorama/Terminal and shared-controller regressions remain in place.
Rendered review at320,390 and1440px found no horizontal overflow in the three
worlds; measured scene, city, support and planner controls remain at least44px.
Chromium's platform-font inspection confirms the original system Helvetica Neue
face is actually used at CSS300, not merely named in a fallback declaration.
The wider photo aperture, saturated graphic field and horizon composition were
reviewed as working pages, along with all three service chapters and footers.

Review corrections included the Signal CTA/footer contrast, Airframe's phone
photo framing and Solari placement, Orbit's compact CTA, aligned scene tabs and
light footer hierarchy. A lifecycle review found that hiding the ornamental
canvas could also prevent physical Solari recalibration and cold-start flips.
The board now calibrates independently of field dimensions. The added real-engine
scenario checks finite city changes, resizing the same cells, no hidden paints
or frame queue, and immediate reduced-motion updates. The full motion suite
passes8/8, including its existing cadence and lifecycle mutation checks.

Final Panorama/visions regression after the motion fix:30/30. The current
iteration also passed Europe11/11, editions20/20, Club flight13/13 and Next12/12:
94 distinct browser scenarios together with the motion suite. Original assertions
remain intact. JavaScript syntax, whitespace, redirect JSON and byte-identical
preservation of the main homepage pass; main f6663503 remains an ancestor.

These are three reviewable hypotheses, not a selected production identity.
Signal is the strongest graphic expression of the existing yellow/black brand;
Airframe prioritizes real-home evidence, and Orbit prioritizes spatial discovery.
Conversion uplift and physical-device frame rate have not been measured.
