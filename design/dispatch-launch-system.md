# One BOOM journey, several native surfaces

7 November 2026. A launch system for the Dispatch direction. This records the
implemented presentation contracts and the remaining production entry work;
it does not declare the public root replaced.

## Visual contract

The entrance uses a petrol midnight surface around the real globe. Black Solari
flaps and BOOM yellow retain their established meaning. White is reserved for
the service/decision ticket and the company chapter. Keep body copy readable;
Helvetica Neue 300 carries destination and product headings, with stronger
weights for actions and essential facts.

Primary actions are solid yellow with dark text, at least 56px high. Utility
controls are at least 44px high. Focus must remain visible on every surface.
The destination directory is a native three-by-two choice group; decorative
movement does not change its state. A tour is explicitly a presentation, not a
customer selection. One visible primary action explains what happens next.

Real prices, availability, service scope, VAT, refunds and application conditions
remain in their native owners. Do not replace factual context with decorative
badges, counts, artificial urgency or generic trust claims. The founder, agency
identity, public conditions and usable product demonstrations provide evidence.

their working architecture rather than rebuilding their forms to match a hero.

   discovery, explicit city links, a real home and a European service.
2. Build the root entry using the same controllers and useful initial HTML;
   preserve all canonical public destinations and agency metadata. Follow the

3. Integrate current main, run the affected journey/truth/SEO checks, then use
   the repository's reviewed PR and main deployment route. Verify the deployed

Visual coherence is checked in the full journey. Conversion and retention are
outcomes to measure after release; they are not established by screenshots or
the number of passing UI checks.
