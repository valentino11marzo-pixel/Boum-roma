# Club, city identity and the European launch

Study: 10 November 2026. This is a Preview refinement and a launch recommendation,
not a production promotion or a measured conversion result.

## Reference and interpretation

Both supplied kxdgraphics reels were viewed in the browser:
- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdtWC-mOjE0/
- https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddl8n0lOp4M/

Observed: geometric London/Big Ben and Warsaw marks in the first reel; Berlin's
TV tower with concentric purple/red/amber construction in the second. Their
70s/80s treatment combines an architectural silhouette, limited colour and
deliberate construction. BOOM adopts that grammar through original geometry,
not the creator's drawings, video or city wordmarks. Helvetica Neue300, black,
ivory and BOOM yellow remain the existing identity.

The six native Flight destinations retain their existing landmarks: Colosseum,
Puerta de Alcalá, Sagrada Família, canal houses, Tower Bridge and the Duomo.
These are editorial city identifiers, not claims of local BOOM offices.
The same city signature becomes legible on phones instead of disappearing.
Its finite response follows an actual city selection; it does not delay a form,
invent availability or compete with the original Solari and globe controllers.
See `club-city-emblems-motion.md` for the gesture and lifecycle contract.

## Where the European launch belongs

The current `/flight-next` and `/flight-studio` are intentionally noindex previews.
The public `/desks` already has readable HTML, city links, service terms, a
self-canonical, metadata and structured data. Its source lives in
`cities/_hub.html`, `cities/_hub.json` and `build-cities.mjs`; do not redesign only
the generated `desks.html` and allow the next generation to erase the work.

For the dedicated European landing, carry the approved Club language into
`/desks`, preserving its eight active foreign destinations and original city
service journeys. Flight's six curated choices include Rome and are not a
replacement for the full network: Paris, Berlin and Valencia must remain.
Keep the city, actual scope, service choices, fees, payment conditions, person
behind BOOM and next step understandable in the initial HTML without canvas.
The globe and city art then enhance that content. A broader root-home redesign
remains the separate launch scope recorded in `dispatch-launch-readiness.md`.

Read-only production checks found HTTP200 for `/`, `/desks` and `/robots.txt`.
The public hub and branch have different update dates; the shared architecture
and eight-city scope are confirmed, not byte parity. Robots allows public pages
and blocks `/api/`; this does not prove that a WAF permits every search crawler.

## Conversion: one test, a real outcome

Hypothesis: quicker recognition of the destination and a clearer next step
increase completed, qualified service requests. Graphic quality may improve
trust, but there is no measured uplift or guaranteed ranking effect yet.

Use the existing consent-gated `js/boom-track.js` and native service events;
do not introduce a second tracker. Existing attribution distinguishes search
and AI referrals and carries first-touch source into lead handling. Establish
the baseline and sample requirements before choosing an experiment duration.

- Primary outcome: confirmed qualified request; completed order where the
  journey supports it. Define the denominator consistently per eligible visitor.
- Diagnose the funnel: destination selection → service review → form start →
  accepted request. Segment by device, city and available acquisition source.
- Treat dwell time, hovers and repeated animation as diagnostics, not success.
- Protect loading and interaction: assess field LCP≤2.5s, INP<200ms and CLS<.1,
  alongside errors, form completion and reduced-motion usability.
- If an alternate URL is used, preserve the original canonical and follow
  temporary testing/redirect guidance. Serve the same truthful content to
  crawlers and people; end the test after enough evidence is available.

This iteration does not activate an experiment, add telemetry or remove noindex.
Preview browser checks are usability evidence, not production conversion data.

## Search and AI discovery

Google says the established SEO practices also apply to its AI features; no
special AI schema or additional optimization is required. Accurate visible
content, crawlable links and indexable pages remain the foundation. OpenAI's
OAI-SearchBot controls search discovery separately from GPTBot training access.
Animations are neither a substitute for those foundations nor a promise of
being cited. Verify the live response, crawler access, canonical and Search
Console render when publishing the actual hub.
