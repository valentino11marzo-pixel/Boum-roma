# Observatory: explore, then choose

10 November 2026. An opt-in Dispatch composition for the user's request for a
more refined, surprising and engaging entrance. The original Solari pulse,
Helvetica Neue300, actual city directory, geography and service journey remain.

Surface mode: Persuade. Exploration helps the visitor understand the real
service and take a deliberate next step.

## Direction

One open geographic stage carries the city word, the real scope and the next
action. The globe extends toward the page edge; a six-city departure deck
anchors the scene. There is no porcelain surround or rounded outer enclosure.
On phones the order is heading, cities, scope/action, then geography, with one
tap to select a city. The established white service tickets and footer follow.

The memorable interaction is inspection before commitment. A deliberate pointer
pause over a city or keyboard focus can preview its original board and camera,
alongside the capability label already present in BOOM's city directory. The
native selected city, budget, dates, currency, service and URL are unchanged.
An explicit Choose-city action or the original city button commits the choice.
There are no new prices, offices, performance figures or demand indicators.

The existing tour owns temporary presentation and its restoration. Manual
inspection cancels the cycling timeout, never starts another recurring loop.
Rapid inspection retargets the same original renderers. Keyboard previews use
no spatial animation. The original globe key handler leaves Tab to the
presentation focus owner, so Choose remains reachable; confirmation returns
focus to the selected native city button. When motion or readiness prevents a paired preview,
only static context is shown; no camera gate is bypassed.

Pause, Escape, leaving the hero, native city choice, another camera action,
navigation, hidden page and offscreen state release the temporary presentation.
The native city click restores presentation before its controller runs, so a
stale snapshot cannot overwrite newly selected copy or service information.
