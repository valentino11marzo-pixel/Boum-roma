## Direction

The black departure scene and the white decision surface have distinct jobs.
The original six-city directory leads to the original Solari, actual photographs
or geographic canvas, and one primary continuation. The outer white hero plate
is removed. The Solari is enlarged on its natural black ground; its existing
mechanics remain the only text animation. No extra background clock is added.
The primary action loses its redundant city-code stub. Quiet proof links remain
available beside it. Photographs retain their real catalogue owner.

The approved BOOM palette remains black #000, white #fff and functional yellow
#ffd700. Secondary text uses the established olive-tinted #c6c9b8 on black and
#535846 on white. Display type stays Helvetica Neue300, with the existing fallback
stack. The white company/footer surface remains the real identity, principles,
contacts and legal information. No new claim about size, reach or conversion.

The existing orthographic atlas is treated as authored geographic material:
its real Natural Earth points use three depth/light bands, the foreground coast
stays legible and the far edge recedes. Selected connections are gold and the
selected pin warm white; decorative glow is removed in Dispatch. This is art
direction, not live sunlight or topography. Connections do not assert flights
or foreign offices. The same canvas, camera and lifecycle remain responsible.
Equivalent longitudes make city/reset/flight targets take the nearest path after
multiple manual rotations. Zoom duration, interruption and accessible controls
remain original; no dependency, polling or animation loop is introduced.
