How it works

  1. Withdraw online above (“Withdraw from contract here”), or send us the form below or any clear statement that you withdraw by email to hello@boom-rome.com, before the 14 days end. The day you paid (for an earlier Paris or Berlin order, the day you ordered) does not count, and a period ending on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday runs to the end of the next working day.
  2. We refund within 14 days of receiving it, to the card you paid with. On a Paris or Berlin order placed before 6 November 2026 nothing is charged before you sign a lease: withdrawing before then ends your order, and you owe nothing.
  3. If you paid and asked us to start before the 14 days ended (with “Start now” at checkout, or later in writing), we deduct only the part of the work already done, in proportion to the price. Once the service is fully performed, the right to withdraw ends.
