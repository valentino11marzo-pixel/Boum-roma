IN BRIEF

Mid-term in Rome, in six facts.

What "mid-term" actually means here
  • 1 to 18 months is its own legal category. Italy's transitional lease (contratto transitorio, art. 5, law 431/98) covers exactly that window: it is registered, it ends on its date, and it protects the tenant like a long lease does.
  • It is not a holiday let. Stays under 30 days are tourist rentals with no registration and no tenant rights; anything longer must be a registered contract with both parties' codice fiscale.
  • Real 2026 prices: studios from about €900, one-bedrooms €1,200–1,800 in central zones, two-bedrooms €1,500–2,400. Furnished, utilities usually extra.
  • The all-in number: monthly rent + a refundable deposit of 2–3 months + BOOM's agency fee of 10% of annual rent. No "administration", "dossier" or viewing fees — ever.
  • You need a reason, and a job or a course is one. The transitional lease requires a documented transitional need: an assignment letter, an enrolment, a project contract all qualify.
  • You can do the whole thing from abroad. Live video viewing, online pre-agreement, deposit by card, contract e-signed — keys on landing day.

THE CONTRACT

The transitional lease, explained in four moves.

1

Duration: 1 to 18 months, and it ends when it says

Unlike the 4+4 long lease, a transitional contract does not auto-renew: it expires on its date, which is what makes landlords willing to sign it for a short stay. Need longer? A new transitional lease can follow if the need persists, or the contract converts to a standard one. Students have their own variant (6 months to 3 years) under the same law. The full comparison: transitorio vs 4+4.

2

The transitional need, in writing

The law wants a reason the stay is temporary — a work assignment, a research grant, a course, a relocation with a known end. The assignment letter or enrolment usually proves it; BOOM tells you exactly which document your case needs before anything is drafted.

3

Registered, or it is not a contract

Every lease over 30 days must be filed with the Agenzia delle Entrate within 30 days of signing, and you must receive the registered copy with its protocol number. In Rome, transitional rents also follow the city's regulated brackets (2023 territorial agreement) — BOOM computes the official figures and handles the attestazione paperwork so the contract is compliant from day one.

4

The paperwork your employer or university will ask for

A registered English-language lease, monthly rent receipts from the tenant portal, and agency fees invoiced by a registered Italian company: the file that expense reports, housing allowances and rental-subsidy schemes want. Company-name leases are possible too — the desk for that is BOOM Executive.

MONTHLY, NOT NIGHTLY

"Monthly rentals in Rome" — the honest version.

Search "monthly rentals Rome" and you land on two very different products. One is a holiday platform charging by the night with a monthly discount: no registered contract, no tenant rights, a price that includes a platform cut and a cleaning fee, and a host who can cancel. The other is a real rental with a monthly cadence — the one you actually want for anything over a month.

At BOOM the rent is billed monthly (or on the cadence you agree), by card or automatic SEPA debit, with a receipt for every payment. The price on the listing is the price: computed to the euro, with rent, deposit and fee shown before you ask. There is no nightly rate hiding inside a monthly number.

FAQ

Asked before every mid-term move.

What is the minimum rental period in Rome?
One month on a transitional lease (art. 5, law 431/98), which runs from 1 to 18 months. Anything under 30 days is a tourist rental — no registration, no tenant protection. BOOM homes are typically let from 3 months up; the listing states the minimum.
Which contract do I sign for a 6-month stay in Rome?
A contratto transitorio: registered with the Agenzia delle Entrate, ending on its date, requiring a documented transitional need (a work assignment or a course is the textbook case). Students can use the dedicated student contract, from 6 months to 3 years.
How much does a mid-term apartment in Rome cost per month?
In 2026, furnished asking prices run roughly €900–1,300 for a studio, €1,200–1,800 for a one-bedroom in central zones and €1,500–2,400 for two bedrooms, with utilities and wifi usually on top (€150–220). Budget a refundable deposit of 2–3 months and the agency fee.
Can I rent a mid-term apartment in Rome from abroad?
Yes — that is the normal path at BOOM, not the exception: live video viewing directed by you, online pre-agreement with typed e-signature, deposit by card or bank transfer, contract e-signed online, keys handed over on landing day.
Can I extend a transitional lease?
The contract ends on its date by design, but if the transitional need continues a new transitional lease can be signed, or the tenancy can move to a standard contract. Tell BOOM early — occupied homes are often reserved months ahead, and your own home is easiest to keep if we know before the end date.
