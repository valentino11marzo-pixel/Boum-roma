IN BRIEF

Furnished, in Rome, in six facts.

Before you compare listings
  • "Furnished" is a contract clause, not a photo. In Italy the lease lists the furniture and appliances included; what is not listed is not owed. Ask for that list before signing, not after.
  • The inventory is what protects your deposit. A signed handover report (verbale di consegna) with the condition of every item is the only thing that decides, a year later, who pays for the sofa.
  • Mid-term homes come furnished by default — nobody ships a bed for eight months. Unfurnished (vuoto) stock exists mostly on 4+4 long leases.
  • Furnished commands a premium of roughly 10–20% over the same flat empty, and the deposit is often the full 3 months the law allows.
  • Real 2026 asking prices: studios €900–1,300, one-bedrooms €1,200–1,800 in central zones, two-bedrooms €1,500–2,400 — utilities and wifi usually extra.
  • Check the boring things: washing machine, heating type and who pays for it, wifi contract in whose name, mattress age. Rome's beautiful apartments hide old boilers.
THE CHECKLIST

Five things to verify in a furnished flat.

1

The inventory, item by item

Every piece of furniture, every appliance, every mark on a wall, written down and signed by both parties at handover. Under art. 1590 of the Civil Code you return the home accounting for normal wear — so "normal" has to be documented on day one. BOOM does the inventory from a filmed walk-through: the list is written from the video, room by room, and attached to the handover report.

2

Appliances that actually work

Test the washing machine, the oven, the hob, the fridge and the boiler during the viewing — on video if you're remote. An appliance that dies of old age is the landlord's to replace (extraordinary maintenance); routine servicing is yours. Get the boiler's age on record if it looks older than a decade. Your rights, clause by clause: the tenant rights cheat sheet.

3

Heating and utilities, in whose name

Central heating in Rome runs on a building schedule (typically November to April, fixed hours); autonomous heating means you control it and pay for it. Utilities transfer to your name with a voltura (€30–80 each), never a new activation; the wifi line stays in the landlord's name only if the contract says so.

4

Photos at move-in, photos at move-out

Dated photos of every room, every appliance, every scratch — at both ends. BOOM films the flat in and out, so the deposit's return is a recording, not a negotiation. If a landlord ever withholds it anyway, the free formal demand letter is where recovery starts.

5

The contract behind the furniture

A furnished flat for 1–18 months goes on a transitional lease, registered with the Agenzia delle Entrate — the only paper that gives you rights. How it works, what it costs and who it's for: mid-term rentals in Rome.

FAQ

Asked about every furnished flat.

What does "furnished" include in an Italian rental?
Whatever the contract's inventory lists — typically bed, wardrobe, sofa, table and chairs, kitchen with hob, oven and fridge, washing machine, and usually linens and kitchenware for mid-term lets. Nothing outside the list is owed, so read it before signing and check it at handover.
Are furnished apartments in Rome more expensive?
Yes, typically 10–20% above the same flat unfurnished, and landlords often ask the full 3-month deposit the law allows. For a stay under two years it is still far cheaper than buying and moving furniture — which is why mid-term homes in Rome come furnished by default.
Who pays if a furnished appliance breaks?
The landlord, when it fails from age or a defect (extraordinary maintenance, art. 1576 c.c.); the tenant, for routine upkeep and damage caused by misuse (art. 1609 c.c.). Record the age and condition of the boiler and white goods in the inventory — it settles the question before it is asked.
Can I remove or store furniture I don't want?
Only with the landlord's written consent, and usually at your cost — the items remain theirs and must come back in the same condition. Ask before signing; a landlord who is flexible in writing is worth more than one who is flexible on the phone.
How do I get my deposit back on a furnished flat?
With evidence: a signed inventory at move-in, dated photos at both ends, and a handover report at move-out. Normal wear is not damage under art. 1590 c.c. If a landlord withholds the deposit anyway, BOOM's free formal demand letter is the first step and our Deposit Recovery service the second.
