BOOM / ATLASLIVE PREVIEW

The strength Skyline is useful from the first screen. Homes, neighbourhoods and daily life become one decision.

The trade-off A map asks more of a first-time visitor. Photography and a clear starting point must do their part.

THE HONEST VERDICT

More identity.
Less ceremony.

The previous giant ROME hero made a strong poster. It did too little to help someone choose a home.

01 / BUILD THE PRODUCT AROUND ATLAS

The city is your advantage.

Skyline can explain the real trade-offs: location, rent, space and the places someone needs to reach. That is a stronger reason to return than a beautiful opening animation.

02 / TAKE THE PHOTOGRAPHY FROM RESIDENCE

A home has to be felt.

Large, honest photographs and clear prices do more for confidence than another badge. This direction also exposes a real dependency: the quality and order of the catalogue photos.

03 / KEEP CLUB FOR THE MOMENTS THAT MATTER

Make the service tangible.

The arrival pass gives BOOM a memorable signature. Use it for services, applications and arrival milestones. Repeating it on every surface would turn a useful idea into decoration.

BOOM / DESIGN STUDY

Three working directions, not three promises of higher conversion. The recommendation is a design judgement; customer testing comes next.
