A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you make your move.

Which Luiss campus should I choose?

Use the address on your teaching timetable. Luiss lists Viale Romania 32, Via Parenzo 11, Viale Pola 12 and Villa Blanc at Via Nomentana 216. Villa Emiliani is at Viale di Villa Emiliani 14. These are separate destinations; a home near one is not automatically near another.

Is Sapienza one campus?

No. The main Città Universitaria is at Piazzale Aldo Moro 5, but teaching also takes place at other sites. This guide includes Architecture at Valle Giulia and Borghese, and Engineering at San Pietro in Vincoli and Castro Laurenziano. Confirm the address for your own classes before comparing homes.

Where are RUFA, Rome Business School and NABA in Rome?

RUFA has a distributed Trieste campus around Via Benaco, plus San Lorenzo at Via degli Ausoni 7 and Ostiense at Via Giuseppe Libetta 7. Rome Business School is at Via Giuseppe Montanelli 5 in Prati. NABA Rome is at Via Ostiense 92. Official campus links are provided in the directory above.

Which Tor Vergata destination should I select?

Choose your faculty, not a generic university centre. Economics is at Via Columbia 2; Engineering at Via del Politecnico 1; Law at Via Cracovia snc; Humanities at Via Columbia 1; Medicine at Via Montpellier 1; Sciences at Via della Ricerca Scientifica 1. Check the exact building and classroom with the university.

How does BOOM choose the homes shown?

Your rent ceiling is applied to current public listings. You choose the order: lower monthly rent, more listed floor area or a shorter estimated journey where travel data exists. The guide shows up to three options and states missing information. It does not invent a matching score, raise your budget or promise availability for your requested stay.

Are the travel times and rental costs guaranteed?

No. Rent is the advertised monthly amount before bills and fees. Travel times are labelled estimates or a timetable model, not live departures. Some campuses have an official address but no verified map point, so no journey estimate is shown. Check the route in Google Maps and confirm dates, terms and full costs on the home enquiry.

What does a shared Your Rome plan contain?

The link contains your rent budget, selected public destination and priority. You can explicitly include up to three public homes and your requested stay. Personal saved places, contact details and device storage are not included. Anyone with the link can read its preferences; prices and availability are checked again when the guide is opened.

What if I need help beyond this catalogue?

Property Finding is BOOM’s search service for people who need help finding a home. Deal Assistance is for people who have already found a home elsewhere and want support with the next steps. Each service page explains its scope, fees and terms before you decide.
